02About

I'm a B.Tech student in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering at Yeshwantrao Chavan College of Engineering, Nagpur. My interest sits at the overlap of wireless systems and software — I like understanding how a signal gets from a sensor to a screen.

In June–July 2025, I attended the 5G Summer School at VNIT Nagpur, where I explored 5G architecture, network protocols, and wireless communication systems, and got hands-on with GNU Radio for signal processing experiments. That experience is what pulled me toward combining wireless/IoT work with the web development I already do.
