Redesigned a quadcopter chassis in SolidWorks, cut mass by 22% using topology optimization while keeping factor of safety above 2.5 under worst-case rotor thrust.

02About

I'm a final-year Mechanical Engineering student at COEP, minoring in Robotics. Most of what I know came from breaking prototypes in the workshop at 11pm before a deadline, not from a textbook — though the textbook usually explains why it broke.

I care about the gap between a CAD model and a thing that actually survives contact with the real world: tolerances, vibration, heat, people who don't read the manual. I want to keep working at that gap, ideally on robotics or hardware-adjacent controls teams.
